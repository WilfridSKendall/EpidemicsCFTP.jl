====== 0 Introduction ======
Created Thursday 24 April 2014

The heart of the idea is a construction of an SIR epidemic using Poisson processes: Section 1 on Unconditional constructions.
These are varied in "algorithmic time" according to a reversible trajectory-valued immigration-death process: Section 2 on Algorithmic Time.
The result is applied to produce a CFTP algorithm (running in algorithmic time): Section 3 on CFTP.
